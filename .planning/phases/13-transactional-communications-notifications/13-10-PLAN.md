<objective>
Map result release, result override, certificate issuance, revocation and reissue, and the learner-facing ticket events to templated mail and notifications that carry ids and references only.

Purpose: COM-01 lists result release, certificate issuance and revocation, and ticket activity. Phases 10 to 12 deliberately emit ids-only payloads (T-11-50 and Phase 12 rules), so each mapper must join to the owning row and copy only allow-listed fields.
Output: the learning mapper group, the completed support mapper group, and unit plus real-Postgres tests.
</objective>

<assumptions>
- A-04: ticket.reopened is a learner action; per D-07 the requester receives the mail and notification, and no assignee alert is added because D-08 does not list one.
- A-18: certificate notifications and mail link to the learner dashboard (where the certificate slot renders), never to the PDF and never to an enrolment page that may no longer be ACTIVE.
- Grades released in a batch emit one event per grade, so a learner with several released assessments gets one mail per grade; correlation is per event by D-05.
- ticket.closed is mailed whether the requester or the auto-close job closed the ticket, because the payload does not distinguish them and D-07 lists closed.
- A ticket-created in-product notification is also created for the requester as the confirmation record (type ticket.created).
</assumptions>
