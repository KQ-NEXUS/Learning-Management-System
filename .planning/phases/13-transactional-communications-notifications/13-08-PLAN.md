<objective>
Route enrolment confirmation, payment failure, refunds and the "payment received, finishing up" notice through the drain as one combined mail per settlement, remove the direct checkout sends, and close the two outbox coverage gaps (payment.failed, payment.refunded).

Purpose: D-07 requires one combined enrolment mail (not two), D-09 requires payment.failed and payment.refunded to exist in the outbox, and COM-02 forbids the current direct send from duplicating the drained mail. This plan adds the mapping first, then removes the old path, so there is never a moment where neither mails.
Output: the enrolment-payment mapper group, the amount formatter, settlement and refund services emitting outbox events, the tightened dispatch contract, and updated unit plus real-Postgres tests.
</objective>

<assumptions>
- A-02: enrolment-confirmed is triggered by enrolment.activated (the seat truth); order details are joined through enrolment.orderId; enrolment.approved (staff approval) reuses the template with no order section.
- A-06: payment.failed and payment.refunded email the learner (payment outcome, COM-01) in the always-sent category; order-payment-exception is kept as the learner mail for order.exception reason illegal_transition, preserving today's behaviour; duplicate_active_enrolment is staff-only (Plan 11).
- A-11: correlation is the event id per D-05; a re-emitted order.exception could yield a duplicate staff mail, accepted and documented (Plan 11); learner mails here are guarded upstream by recordWebhookEventOrSkip.
- Amount labels use a fixed "en" locale so output does not depend on the server default locale; the previous unspecified-locale formatter is replaced by this helper.
- A Paystack payment failure has no existing FAILED transition in the settlement service, so payment.failed is emitted from the one existing FAILED transition (recordPaymentFailureAsSystem); no new failure path is invented.
- refund-service.ts currently performs the refund update, order update and audit outside a transaction; the completion writes move into one transaction, the audit call stays after commit as today.
</assumptions>
