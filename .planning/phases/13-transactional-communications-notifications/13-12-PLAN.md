<objective>
Give Global Administrators a read-only delivery log with an audited Resend, reusing the dispatch row.

Purpose: COM-01 requires a delivery record per email and D-06 requires operators to see status, attempts and errors and to resend a failed or lost email with an accountable reason. The log is also where the failed-email administrator alert (Plan 11) points.
Output: the permission-gated log service, the staff page with its table, dialog and action, the sidebar entry, and unit, real-Postgres and component tests.
</objective>

<assumptions>
- A-05: the log is gated by audit.view at global scope (an empty resource scope, as the audit page does) and Resend by users.manage at global scope; no catalogue identifier is added. This should be confirmed with the user because the closest existing permissions were chosen for the "Global Administrators" audience.
- The recipient address is shown in full to audit.view holders because diagnosis needs it and the page is administrator-only; the UI-SPEC's "reference" column is rendered as the address in monospace.
- The list is capped at the newest 100 rows with status and template filters and no paging in this phase.
- Historical rows with the legacy template id order-confirmation, and any unknown template string, render as their raw string in the log and are never resendable (no stored params).
- Resending a SENT row deliberately sends a second copy; the dialog copy says so and the audit entry records why.
</assumptions>
