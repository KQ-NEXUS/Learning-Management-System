<objective>
Deliver the staff side: permission- and scope-resolved alerts (new tickets, submissions, payment exceptions), the assignee and escalation emails, and the administrator alert when an email finally fails.

Purpose: COM-03 requires notifications for staff actions, and D-20 requires that nobody is alerted about a record they cannot open. Resolution must run inside the off-request drain, so it cannot use the request-scoped permission layer; a SQL resolver with a parity test against the real authorization core keeps the two in step.
Output: the staff recipient resolver, the staff mapper group, the failed-email alert service wired into the drain, and unit plus real-Postgres tests.
</objective>

<assumptions>
- A-03: ticket.escalated resolves the target from Ticket.assigneeId at drain time; with no assignee it alerts in-product only, to global tickets.manage holders, and sends no email fan-out.
- A-05 and A-12: the failed-email alert goes to global audit.view holders (the people who can open the delivery log), only for drain-path Pass 2 failures; in-request auth-mail failures raise no alert.
- A-11: staff exception mail correlation is the event id per D-05, so a producer that re-emits order.exception for one incident can send a second staff mail; accepted and documented (Pitfall 3).
- Tickets are authorised globally (empty scope) in the existing services, so ticket alerts go to global tickets.manage holders, matching the existing listTicketAssignees precedent (staff, ACTIVE, global assignment).
- The reason-label allow-list covers the coded order.exception reasons emitted today (amount_or_currency_mismatch, payment_attempt_not_found, illegal_payment_transition, illegal_transition, duplicate_active_enrolment, no_order, attempt_not_found); anything else renders "Payment needs review".
- Staff recipients require users.isStaff true and status ACTIVE in addition to the assignment predicates.
- Alert flooding during a provider outage is bounded by the Pass 2 batch of 25 per run and accepted (low).
</assumptions>
