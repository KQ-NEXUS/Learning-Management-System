<objective>
Build the export half of the D-12 privacy tool: one learner's data prepared as a file through the export job system that already exists, with nothing in it that belongs to someone else.

Purpose: NFR-11 requires support for access and export of a person's data; NFR-13 requires long-running work to expose queued, processing, succeeded, failed and retrying states. The ExportJob pipeline (RPT-04) already has those states, retry, a short-lived download and audit (RESEARCH "Don't Hand-Roll"), so the privacy export is a new dataset and producer, not a new job system.

Output: the privacy export service and producer, the dataset definition, the producer registration, the licence registry entry and an integration test with exclusion proofs.
</objective>
