<objective>
Remove the three performance risks the research verified in code before any load is measured: the staff support queue that reads the whole ticket table, the staff layout's repeated session and grant reads, and the unbounded staff payments list.

Purpose: NFR-02 requires common authenticated views to reach usable content within 2.5 s at p75 under 150 learners and 10 staff (D-02). The staff layout runs about 16 can() checks per page, each reading the session and the grants (RESEARCH A2 slow spot 1); the queue (folded todo) and payments list grow with data. The measured effect is recorded in the local rehearsal (plan 15-28) with pg_stat_statements; this plan makes the changes and proves behaviour is unchanged.

Output: the SQL-backed queue with a differential test, request-scoped dedupe with a revocation test, and a paged payments list.

Shared file note: src/app/staff/layout.tsx is not edited; dedupe happens underneath it.
</objective>
