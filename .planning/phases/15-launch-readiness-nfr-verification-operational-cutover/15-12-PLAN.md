Prove with concurrent tests that grading, completion, webhook replay and quiz attempts tolerate retries without duplicate business effects, and add the two database constraints the research found missing.

Output: four concurrency integration tests, one additive migration with two partial unique indexes, service conflict handling, and the [BLOCKING] local schema push.

Go-live note: plan 15-31 adds this migration to the ordered Neon list with its diagnostic SELECTs, run before applying.
</objective>
