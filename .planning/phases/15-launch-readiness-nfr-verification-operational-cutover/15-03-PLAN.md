<objective>
Extend rate limiting beyond sign-in to the five public or learner entry points D-11 names, reusing the LoginThrottle table and the sign-in throttle's atomic upsert, with neutral messages that reveal nothing about accounts.

Purpose: NFR-04 lists rate limiting in the security baseline; today only sign-in is throttled, and register, forgot password and resend verification are only per-address cooled down. Serverless instances share no memory, so the counter lives in Postgres. Each refusal is audited (NFR-07) with a correlation id.

Output: a generalised limiter, its service binding and licence classification, the five wired entry points with UI-SPEC S5 copy, and unit, integration, action and DOM-shape tests.

Edge coverage disposition: NFR-04 boundary and precision rows are resolved as explicit truths above (limit N and N+1, the exact window edge, minutes rounding). Prohibitions P4 and P7 are authored descriptor-less above.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-15-03-01 | Denial of service | register, forgot, resend actions (mail bombing, sign-up floods) | high | mitigate | Per-device DB-backed limits plus the existing per-address 60 s cooldown; atomic counter proven under 40 concurrent attempts |
| T-15-03-02 | Information disclosure | account enumeration through limiter responses | high | mitigate | Keys and counts identical for known and unknown addresses; per-address cooldown stays silent; integration test compares known and unknown results |
| T-15-03-03 | Information disclosure | certificate reference scraping | medium | mitigate | 60 checks per device per 15 minutes; the throttled branch performs no lookup and renders no definition list |
| T-15-03-04 | Spoofing | client IP header | medium | mitigate | Only clientIpFrom (Netlify header or TRUSTED_CLIENT_IP_HEADER) is trusted; no IP falls back to per-address keys, never a shared bucket |
| T-15-03-05 | Repudiation | refused attempts | low | mitigate | rate_limit.blocked audit row with IP and correlationId and no personal free text |
| T-15-03-06 | Denial of service | shared NAT addresses at cohort start | medium | mitigate | Loose per-device limits (30 to 60) and per-learner ticket limit; numbers recorded in ledger A13 for owner redirect |
