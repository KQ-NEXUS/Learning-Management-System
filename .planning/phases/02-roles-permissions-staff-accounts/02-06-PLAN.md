<objective>
Build the staff-account lifecycle: create an account together with its first role assignment in one
transaction, deactivate it with a reason and immediate session revocation, and reactivate it as a
pure status flip.

Purpose: IAM-04 is the last service-layer requirement in this phase, and it is the one that touches
credentials, sessions, and the continuity guard at once. Deactivation in particular has three
distinct obligations that must not drift apart — a reason, a session sweep, and the
last-administrator check — and only one of the three is visible from the database schema.

Output: `staff-account-service.ts` and its test file, both running without a database.
</objective>
