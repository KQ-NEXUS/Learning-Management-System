<objective>
Emit licence notices as deduplicated domain events and map them to in-product notifications and staff emails for Global `licence.view` holders through the existing Phase 13 drain.

Purpose: LIC-07 requires Administrators to be told about renewal, grace, expiry, invalid and validation-attention states without duplicates or sensitive detail (D-15). Tracer: a grace-state snapshot emits one event and the mapper fans out a notification and an email per holder.

Output: `licence-notice-service.ts`, `event-mappers/licence.ts`, the mapper registration and three test files. The scheduled task that calls the notice service is plan 14-16.

Interface contract (do not rename): `licenceNoticeService.emitNotices({ snapshot: LicenceStatusSnapshot; noticeKeys: string[] }): Promise<{ created: string[]; skipped: string[] }>` (event ids); `createLicenceMappers(): MapperGroup`; event payload fields `licenceId` (string or "none"), `noticeKey`, `state`, optional `days`, `expiry`, `graceEnd`, `reasonCode` (strings).

Edge coverage: LIC-07 (unclassified, flagged assumption): the probe returned "review manually"; this plan assumes the acceptance outcome "messages are deduplicated and do not leak sensitive signing or contract details" is met by deterministic event ids plus the three existing unique layers, the closed copy set and the allow-listed params, and is proven by the drain integration test.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|

| T-14-14-02 | Information disclosure | signing, contract or deployment detail in a notice | high | mitigate | Closed copy set, field-by-field allow-listed payload and params, content-prohibition tests over payload, params, email and rendered text |
| T-14-14-03 | Denial of service | notification or email flooding | medium | mitigate | Deterministic event ids dedupe at three layers; only the current threshold bucket is emitted; replay test asserts unchanged counts |
