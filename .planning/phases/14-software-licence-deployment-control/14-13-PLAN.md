<objective>
Implement the D-08 payment rule: payments initiated before the restriction instant complete and receive their entitlement, anything later becomes a visible exception with the money recorded, while webhooks, reconciliation and refunds stay operational.

Purpose: avoid "charged but undelivered" in restricted continuity mode without keeping new commercial use open. Tracer: one webhook for an attempt initiated before the cutoff activates and one initiated beyond the tolerance becomes the new exception, at the service level with the real rule.

Output: the rule in `checkout-webhook-system-service.ts`, the staff label, and unit and integration tests.

Interface contract: `SettlementDeps.licence?: { getRestrictionCutoff(): Promise<{ restricted: boolean; restrictedAt: Date | null }> }` (plan 14-09 `licenceService` satisfies it); new activation result reason `"payment_after_restriction"`; tolerance constant `SKEW_TOLERANCE_MS` (600000) from `@/server/licence/constants`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| provider webhook to settlement | Unauthenticated server-to-server events settle money; the licence rule must not drop or duplicate payments |
| licence state to settlement | A wrong cutoff either blocks paid orders or activates unpaid commercial use |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-14-13-01 | Tampering | charged but undelivered | high | mitigate | Attempts initiated within cutoff plus 10 minutes activate; later ones record SUCCEEDED money, flag the Order and open a reconciliation case so staff can refund; integration tests for every branch |
| T-14-13-02 | Tampering | TOCTOU between guard check and attempt row insert | medium | mitigate | 10-minute tolerance on the comparison; boundary tests at exactly the tolerance and 1 ms beyond |
| T-14-13-03 | Denial of service | transient licence read failure rejects a paid order | high | mitigate | getRestrictionCutoff failure falls back to not restricted; unit test for the failure path |
| T-14-13-04 | Elevation of privilege | new commercial use through manual confirmation after restriction | medium | mitigate | Manual path anchored on Order.createdAt; later orders cannot be created (checkout blocked, plan 14-12) and any that exist are exceptions |
| T-14-13-05 | Information disclosure | staff label reveals licence state to cohort-scoped finance staff | low | mitigate | Neutral fixed label; test asserts no licence, restricted or expiry wording |
