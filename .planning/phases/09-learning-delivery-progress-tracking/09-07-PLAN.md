<objective>
Build the aggregate read behind `/dashboard`: for each of the learner's own ACTIVE enrolments, the
derived next action, the two progress components, the nearest upcoming sessions, the access-window
notice, and the four forward-pointing named gaps.

Purpose: LRN-01 names seven things a learner must see. Three of them (assessment obligations,
results, tickets) and one more (certificate state) belong to Phases 10/12/11. Typing them as named
gaps now is what stops a fake zero appearing and what lets each later phase widen one field instead
of restructuring this service.
Output: `enrolment-dashboard-service.ts` and its unit tests.
</objective>
