<objective>
Move Cohort administration and publication readiness from one price and one currency to two
independent administrator-entered base prices, with per-rail readiness replacing the single price
check.

Purpose: the tracer proved the read path — `startCheckout` reading `priceNgnMinor`. This plan builds
the write path an administrator actually uses, and the publication gate that stops a Cohort going
live with an enabled rail nobody can pay on (D-08, COH-02). It is the first expansion outward from
the proven slice.
Output: a dual-price Cohort form, validated server-side, with two readiness items and two detail facts.
</objective>
