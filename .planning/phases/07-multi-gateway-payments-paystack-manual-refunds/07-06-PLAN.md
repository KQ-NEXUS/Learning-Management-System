<objective>
Convert the Phase 6 Stripe Checkout Session from a direct platform charge into a Connect destination
charge, so the USD rail settles the school's immutable base price to its connected account while KQ
NEXUS bears Stripe's actual fee.

onto the same snapshot-and-split contract by changing only the session-creation parameters — the
webhook, settlement service, seat accounting, consent capture and receipt path are all already
correct and must stay untouched.

</objective>
