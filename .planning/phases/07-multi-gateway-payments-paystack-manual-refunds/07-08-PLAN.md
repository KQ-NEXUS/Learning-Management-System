<objective>
Add the two staff-initiated money paths — manual payment confirmation and refunds — on top of the
proven settlement transition, with the duplicate guard, the eligible-value cap, and the component
allocation Finance needs to reconcile a reversal.

Purpose: PAY-03, PAY-04, PAY-05 and PAY-13 are the only phase requirements not reachable through a
provider webhook. They are also the highest-privilege operations in the system: a manual confirmation
moves an enrolment to ACTIVE with no provider evidence behind it, and a refund moves real money back.
Both must route through the same choke points everything else does.
Output: two permission-gated services, two isolated provider refund calls, and their proofs.
</objective>

Allocation rules, all integer arithmetic:
- The cap is the learner total minus the sum of already-recorded refunds; exceeding it is refused
  with the eligible figure named in the error (PAY-05, D-22).

refunding must not implicitly revoke access and revoking must not implicitly refund. A provider call
that fails leaves the refund in a non-COMPLETED `RefundStatus` with the provider's error recorded; it

| T-07-36 | Tampering | duplicate manual confirmation | high | mitigate | PAY-04 guard reads the Order first and returns the existing transaction without creating anything; settlement goes through `activateOrderAsSystem`'s terminal-state guard as a second line of defence (PAY-04, PAY-11) |
