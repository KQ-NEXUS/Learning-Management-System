## Decisions Made

- **Display h1 sizes kept.** `text-[28px]` (catalogue index) and `text-[33px]` (catalogue
  detail) are the exact display-scale roles in UI-SPEC 4.3 lines 348–350, reused nowhere
  the primitive scale applies. Left as-is; the mechanical checker permits them on
  non-primitive files.
- **D-23 over UI-SPEC 7.3.** UI-SPEC 7.3 prose snaps the learner top bar to `16px 24px`;
  CONTEXT D-23 and the design-contract `LOCKED_EXCEPTIONS` keep it at `14px 28px`. CONTEXT
  wins — applied `px-7 py-3.5`, and extended `px-7` to the mobile-nav panel for alignment
  (both tokens are file-scoped allow-listed).
- **Public 404 CTA consolidated.** The approved UI-SPEC 6.2 design has one CTA. The prior
  public page had two ("Browse courses" / "Browse programmes"); collapsed to the single
  "Back to the catalogue" → `/courses`. `/courses` is the catalogue root, so no
  destination capability is lost.
- **`404` mono eyebrow retained.** The mockup's not-found panel omits it; it is not
  record-sensitive and removing DOM content is discouraged under the locked boundary.
  Flagged for the plan-29 rendered review.
- **`src/app/account/page.tsx` not modified.** It already uses correct roles; touching it
  would be churn with no role effect.
