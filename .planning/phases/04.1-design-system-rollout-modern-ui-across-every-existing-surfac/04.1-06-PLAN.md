<objective>
Replace six copies of the same centred-column wrapper with one shared split-panel auth shell, and
move every auth field onto a control edge a low-vision user can see.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 asks for one shared `(auth)/layout.tsx` instead of six repetitions.
D-21 raises the bar from "lift the shared string" to "build the drawn split panel", and D-22
supersedes Phase 3's decision to keep these screens chrome-free — the intent behind that decision
was that the six screens must not drift apart from each other, and one shared layout satisfies it far
more strongly than a bare column ever did.

Output: a new guard-free `(auth)/layout.tsx`, a small shared content-helper module, six pages
carrying only their own content, and five form components on 3:1 control edges.
</objective>

  seventh occurrence and is deleted by plan 07, which is what takes the repo-wide count to zero.

  change completely by design. The structural done-condition above replaces it.
