## Planner Assumptions

- **Locator trail is section-level, not record-level — a deliberate divergence from UI-SPEC 7.1.**
  UI-SPEC section 7.1 draws breadcrumbs in the header bar and section 8.17 describes a deep trail
  with collapsing middle crumbs. `DetailLayout` already renders record-level breadcrumbs inside the
  content, and the shell cannot resolve a record id to a name without a per-route data fetch. The
  plan therefore renders a two-crumb section trail in the shell and leaves record-level breadcrumbs
  to `DetailLayout`. Section 8.17's overflow and long-text rules are satisfied trivially by a
  two-crumb fixed-literal trail, and its load-bearing `error` requirement — that the trail keeps
  showing the attempted route when a child errors — is satisfied more strongly by a shell-level
  trail than by a page-level one.
- **The identity chip has no role label to render.** `Actor` is `{ userId, isStaff? }` and
  `ProfileSnapshot` carries no role. UI-SPEC 8.17's own degradation rule covers this: omit the line
  entirely rather than reserve its height. No new role lookup is introduced for chrome.
- **The decorative search chip is in tension with D-20.** D-20 removes disabled nav placeholders so
  nothing promises a page that does not exist; UI-SPEC 7.1 nonetheless draws a cosmetic search chip.
  The plan follows UI-SPEC and renders it non-interactive and `aria-hidden`, which removes the
