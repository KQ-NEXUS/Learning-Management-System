Permit-to-work systems

A permit to work is a formal written authorisation to carry out a defined task, under defined controls, for a defined period. It is not paperwork for its own sake — it is the record that a competent person considered the hazard before anyone was exposed to it.

Key points A permit names the person accountable, not just the task. It expires — an open permit past its window is a finding, not a formality.
