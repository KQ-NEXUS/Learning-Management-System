<objective>
Move the four public catalogue routes and the two 404 pages onto the approved system — card grids,
the new brand blue, the detail type scale — without touching the properties that keep an unpublished
record invisible.

Purpose: this is the front-of-house half of ROADMAP criterion 5. It is also the part of the phase
with the most to lose: these routes are the only ones a stranger can reach, and their 404 and
detail-lookup behaviour is what stops a private course being discoverable by URL.

Output: two card-grid indexes, two restyled detail pages, and two 404 panels that still say nothing.
</objective>

  kept and the divergence is recorded rather than silently resolved.
