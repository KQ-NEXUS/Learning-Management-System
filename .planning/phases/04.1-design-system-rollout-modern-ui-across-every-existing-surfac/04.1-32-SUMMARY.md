## Decisions Made

- catch messages are fixed generic sentences (no caught value interpolated), matching the `ModuleComposer` `ADD_FALLBACK_ERROR` style.
- The stale flag stays `false` on a rejection — an unknown outcome is not a STALE optimistic-concurrency conflict.
- No retry loop / timer / toast; recovery is user-triggered re-activation only.
