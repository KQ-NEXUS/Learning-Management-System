### Findings from the in-progress walkthrough

- **[2026-09-07] Staff sidebar scrolled out of view on tall routes — FIXED, needs re-check.**
  On `/staff/courses`, `/staff/audit` etc. the desktop navy sidebar slid up and
  off-screen as the page scrolled. Root cause: `overflow-x: hidden` on
  `StaffShell.tsx`'s flex-row wrapper forced `overflow-y` to compute to `auto`,
  so the sidebar's `lg:sticky` anchored to that box instead of the viewport.
  Fix in commit `344c3a4` — overflow clip moved to the content column, sidebar
  given `overflow-y-auto`, 3 class-contract regressions added to
  `staff-shell.test.tsx`. Re-verify in a browser: scroll a long staff list at
  ≥1024px and confirm the sidebar stays pinned full-height; also check the
  footer identity chip is not clipped at a short viewport.
