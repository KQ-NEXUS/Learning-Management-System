## Concrete unresolved captions decision

Evidence read from current source:
- Lesson has body/embedUrl/linkUrl, no captions property.
- LessonResource has file metadata and scan status, no video-caption/language association.
- VIDEO upload allow-list in src/lib/upload-limits.ts accepts video/mp4 and video/webm only.
- LessonMediaPlayer has only src/title props and an empty track; its comment explicitly anticipated future caption authoring.

Therefore a real authorized WebVTT source cannot be supplied by a styling edit or by assuming a title/body is a transcript. Proposed narrow next option for user review: introduce a scanned WebVTT resource associated with a video, language/label metadata, staff authoring selection, authorized delivery and publication-readiness policy. This requires new upload/data contracts and potentially schema migration. Exact schema and backwards compatibility must be planned after scope approval; none is silently authorized here. Disabling publication or hiding working video would remove an existing capability and is rejected under the locked boundary.

Plan 23 removes misleading source-less track markup while preserving native playback and download. This is partial remediation only, not CR-11 closure. Plan 29 reports the decision and retains NFR-09 pending. The plan set is executable for in-scope repairs but does not claim complete accessibility coverage.
