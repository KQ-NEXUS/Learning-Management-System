  <what-built>

    timezone-correct storage, repeat-weekly and a gated meeting link (COH-03), the enrolment state machine with

    restriction and a post-window correction path (ATT-01/02/03).

    Everything automatable is already automated and green: 6 integration/schema suites against real Postgres
    (including the two-concurrent-take-seat capacity-1 proof), the full unit and component suites, lint, types
    and a production Docker build. What remains genuinely needs a human at the running app.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    The stack from Task 1 is running, migrated and seeded. Sign in as a staff account with GLOBAL grants
    unless a step says otherwise.

    1. **Readiness panel and publish dialog (COH-04).** Open `/staff/cohorts` and pick a DRAFT cohort. On the
       Overview tab, confirm the Publication readiness panel lists the six categories in the order
       Catalogue → Schedule → Price → Capacity → Instructors → Completion, that each state is shown by a glyph
       AND a text label (never colour alone), and that any `NOT_YET_CHECKED` item renders the `•` third state
       rather than a grey tick or cross. Create the failing condition deliberately — for an INSTRUCTOR_LED
       cohort, remove its instructor, or set capacity to 0. Confirm the panel shows FAIL for that item and the
       **Publish cohort** button is disabled. Fix the condition, reload, and confirm the panel refreshes to
       PASS and the button enables. Finally open the publish dialog and confirm it summarises the same items
       the panel showed.

    2. **Meeting-link visibility window (COH-03 / D-25).** On the Sessions tab, confirm the list shows only
       whether a link is configured and never the URL itself. Then, using a session whose start is further
       away than its `linkVisibleFromMinutes`, view it as an ENROLLED learner and confirm the link is hidden
       with the "The meeting link appears {n} minutes before the session starts." message. Edit that session's
       start time so it falls inside the window, reload as the same learner, and confirm the link now appears.
       Finally view the same session as a signed-in user who is NOT enrolled in that cohort and confirm the
       link is hidden at every point in time.

    3. **Bulk attendance save (ATT-01).** Open a cohort with several enrolled learners and pick a session that
       has ALREADY STARTED. On the attendance screen, change several learners' states, confirm a dirty
       indicator appears and that navigating away warns you first, then press **Save attendance** once.
       Confirm every changed learner now shows the new state after reload and that unchanged learners were not
       stamped. Separately, open a session that has NOT yet started and confirm present/absent/late are
       disabled with the "You can only mark excused or not-recorded before the session starts." hint, while
       excused and not-recorded remain selectable.

    4. **Post-window correction (ATT-03).** Pick a session whose marking window has closed (ended more than
       7 days ago — adjust a seeded session's dates if needed). Change one learner's state and confirm a
       danger confirmation appears requiring a reason of at least 10 characters, that its description names
       the old and new state, and that after saving the roster shows the record as a correction. Open
       `/staff/audit` and confirm both the original mark and the correction appear with before/after values,
       the actor and the reason.

    5. **Cohort-cancel bulk withdraw (D-31).** Pick a cohort that has active enrolments. Press **Cancel
       cohort**, confirm the dialog states how many active enrolments will be withdrawn and requires a
       reason, then confirm. Afterwards check: the cohort status is CANCELLED, its seats-taken reads 0, every
       previously active learner shows WITHDRAWN on the roster with your reason, every open session shows as
       cancelled, and `/staff/audit` contains one entry PER withdrawn learner — not a single batch entry.
       Confirm nothing was deleted: the cohort, its sessions and its enrolments are all still listed.

    6. **Hold-expiry sweep (COH-06 / D-03).** The seed includes a `PENDING_PAYMENT` enrolment whose hold has
       already expired. Within about five minutes of the worker starting, confirm from the worker logs that
       it released expired holds, then confirm on the roster that the enrolment is CANCELLED with the reason
       "hold expired", that the cohort's seats-taken decreased by one, and that `/staff/audit` shows the
       cancellation attributed to SYSTEM rather than to a user.

    7. **Exceptions CSV parity (ATT-04).** On the Exceptions tab, apply a category filter and note the row
       count on screen. Download the CSV and confirm it contains exactly that many data rows and the same
       learners. Change the filter and repeat once.

    8. **Keyboard and denial spot-check (NFR-09 / RBAC-06).** Tab through the cohort detail tabs using the
       arrow keys, Home and End, and confirm focus is always visible. Then sign in as an Instructor granted
       only one cohort and confirm: their assigned cohort's roster opens, and a direct URL to a different
       cohort's detail returns a not-found page that reveals nothing about that cohort.

    Report any deviation from the copy or behaviour above rather than accepting a close-enough match — the
    copy is a contract fixed in 05-UI-SPEC.md.
  </how-to-verify>
  <resume-signal>Type "approved" to close Phase 5, or describe each issue found.</resume-signal>
