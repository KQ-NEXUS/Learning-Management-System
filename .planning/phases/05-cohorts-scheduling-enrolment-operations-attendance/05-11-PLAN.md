<objective>
Complete the Cohort lifecycle with the cancel operation — a bulk withdraw under one shared reason plus a
status change, all atomic — and extend the seed so every Phase-5 screen has real populated and exception
states to render.

Purpose: D-31 makes cohort cancellation a high-impact integrity action: a Cohort is never deleted, and
cancelling one with learners on it must withdraw them explicitly and audit each transition, not orphan
them. The seed work matters because six render states per screen cannot be verified against an empty
database, and the exception views need a cohort that actually has a missing register and a corrected record.
Output: `cancelCohort` in `cohort-service.ts`, an integration proof, and extended `prisma/seed.ts`.
</objective>
