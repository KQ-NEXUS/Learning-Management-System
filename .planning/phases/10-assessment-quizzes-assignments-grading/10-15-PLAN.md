Ship ASM-07's learner-facing results surface: a dedicated results page, and the two Phase 9 dashboard
slots (`assessmentObligations` and `results`) widened from their deferred placeholders to real data.

dashboard would be structurally incapable of rendering a fake zero until this phase landed. Widening
