<objective>
Prove the phase's four riskiest invariants against real infrastructure rather than in-memory fakes:
the verified-upload receipt, Cohort-scoped grading denial, batch-release atomicity, and the
released-only learner read.

Purpose: Every unit test in this phase drives its service with fakes. A fake cannot raise a real
unique-constraint violation, cannot prove a `$transaction` rolls back an outbox row alongside a grade
update, cannot prove a `HeadObject` mismatch against a real object store, and cannot prove a
COHORT-scoped grant is denied rather than silently filtered. Docker with Postgres, MinIO and ClamAV
is confirmed healthy in this environment, so these suites are expected to actually run — not to be
deferred as human-only verification the way several Phase 6 integration tests were.
Output: three real-infrastructure integration suites.
</objective>
