<objective>
Close REG-05: the learner sees a truthful confirmation while the webhook is in flight, then a permanent
receipt carrying all six required fields, and gets exactly one confirmation email.

Purpose: this is where the phase either tells the truth or does not. The happy case is easy — money
moved, seat activated, show a success page. The case that decides whether the receipt is trustworthy is
the one plan 06-06 proved is real: the payment succeeded and the enrolment did not, because the hold was
swept while the learner was on Stripe's page. A confirmation page that says "You're enrolled" in that
state is lying to someone who has been charged, and it is the easiest lie in the codebase to write by
accident, because the exception path is rare and the success template is right there.

The amount is the second place truth can quietly slip. The receipt must show what was charged, which is
the figure recorded on the Order when it was created — not the cohort's price today. Re-reading the live
price would make a receipt that silently rewrites itself when someone edits a cohort, which is the
opposite of what a receipt is for.

Third: exactly one email. `dispatchBestEffort` exists so a Brevo outage cannot fail the webhook's 200
back to Stripe, and plan 06-06's replay guard is what stops a redelivered event from sending a second
copy. Both properties have to hold together — the send is best-effort in one direction and exactly-once
in the other.
Output: the confirmation email, the interstitial, and the receipt with both its sub-states.
</objective>
